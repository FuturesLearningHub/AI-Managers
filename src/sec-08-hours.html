Together

Everyone picks a habit and says it out loud to the table, with the day and time it will happen: “Friday triage, 3:30, before I leave.” A habit with a slot on the calendar survives; a habit with only good intentions does not. Tables note who picked what, so there is someone to ask in two weeks.
