Glossary

Every technical word this course used.

One line each, in the order you met them.

  • AI tool. Software you type an instruction into and get written work back. Amplify, Copilot, and ChatGPT EDU are the three Vanderbilt approves.
  • Prompt. The instruction you type. Everything in this course is about writing better ones.
  • Model. The system underneath the tool that produces the text. When this course says the model sounds confident, it means the writing style, not a claim about accuracy.
  • Data level. Vanderbilt’s label for how sensitive information is, from 1 (public) to 4 (most restricted).
  • PHI. Protected health information: anything about a person’s health that could identify them.
  • HIPAA. The federal law governing protected health information.
  • SSO. Single sign-on: logging in with your Vanderbilt account rather than a separate password.
  • Custom assistant, custom GPT. A saved setup that starts every chat with instructions you wrote once.
  • Transcript. The text record a meeting tool makes of what was said.
  • Premortem. Assuming a decision has already failed and writing the reasons, so the risks surface before you commit.
  • Second-order effect. What happens because of what happens: the consequence of the consequence.
  • Situation, Behavior, Impact. A feedback structure: what the moment was, what the person did, what followed.
  • Reinforcing and redirecting feedback. Feedback that asks for more of something, and feedback that asks for something different.
  • Stretch goal. A goal set slightly beyond current reach, used in development conversations.
  • Triage. Sorting a pile by what it needs rather than working through it in order.
